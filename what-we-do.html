Supporting military families

Our purpose.

Our sole purpose is to provide free holidays for military personnel, veterans, and families with a military background. Those who would benefit from this opportunity are put in touch with us by various military charities around the UK, such as Give Us Time.

We organise social events and attend local events with our display of military-related items. All monies raised by us are used to give our guests a relaxing week’s holiday in a luxury caravan at Riverside Caravan Centre, who have been wonderful in supporting our aims.

We also give our visitors a welcome hamper, cuddly toys for younger children, and a Tesco voucher. A bottle of bubbly is supplied for the grown-ups too!

All our funding comes from our organised social events, which are open to everybody, and from donations.
